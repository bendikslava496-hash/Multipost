MultiPost

A private publishing tool that schedules and uploads short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Connects YouTube channels with Google OAuth, authorized by the channel owner.
  • Uploads videos to YouTube Shorts on a schedule set by the owner.
  • Helps draft titles, descriptions and hashtags, which the owner reviews before publishing.
  • Sends status notifications to the owner through a private Telegram bot.

Who it is for

MultiPost is operated by its owner for managing their own channels. It is not offered as a public service and does not allow sign-ups.

Use of Google data

MultiPost uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos and read basic channel information for channels the owner has connected, and the TikTok Content Posting API only to send approved videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts as drafts. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
