Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how MultiPost ("the app") handles information. MultiPost is a private tool operated by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It does not offer public accounts or sign-ups.

1. Information the app accesses

When a channel owner connects a YouTube channel through Google OAuth, the app receives:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for that channel;
  • basic channel information: channel ID, channel title and thumbnail;
  • status of videos uploaded by the app (for example, video ID and processing state).

The app also stores the videos, photos, titles, descriptions and hashtags that the owner submits for publishing, and a log of publishing actions.

2. Google API scopes

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos and set their thumbnails;
  • youtube.readonly — to read channel details and the status of uploaded videos.

3. How information is used

Information obtained from Google APIs is used only to upload videos the owner has approved, to show channel and upload status to the owner, and to schedule publications. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

4. Compliance with Google API policies

MultiPost's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. MultiPost uses YouTube API Services; by connecting a channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share information obtained from Google APIs with third parties. Data is not transferred to anyone except Google itself, as part of uploading videos through the YouTube Data API.

6. Storage and security

Data is stored on a private server controlled by the owner. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. The admin interface is not exposed to the public internet. Logs do not contain tokens.

7. Retention and deletion

  • OAuth tokens are kept while a channel is connected and are deleted immediately when the channel is disconnected in the app.
  • Channel information and publishing logs are deleted within 30 days after a channel is disconnected.
  • Uploaded media files are deleted from the server after publishing or cancellation, within 30 days.

8. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the channel in the app or at Google Account → Third-party connections. After revocation the app can no longer access the channel.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
